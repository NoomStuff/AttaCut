# AttaCut

Cut your clips, move on.
AttaCut is a fast video trimmer for getting clips out of a recording with as little effort as possible.

Quickly cutting or trimming a recording takes way more effort than it should. I wanted to just open a recording, drag the handles to the sections I actually picked, and get out. So that's the whole app.
